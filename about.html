Our story

We built what we wished existed.

Most Australian businesses grow on referrals until they stop scaling. The ceiling feels like a market problem. It is almost always a pipeline problem.

Our founder lived through it first-hand, building a telecommunications business from the ground up. The lesson was simple. Growth without a system is luck. And luck eventually runs out.

Automate Accelerator was built to solve that. Not as an agency that takes a retainer and sends a monthly report. As a partner that builds, runs and owns the outbound system on behalf of the business.

Every client has skin in the game. So do we.

What makes us different

What we do. And what we do not.

The Australian market is full of growth companies promising the same thing. We are not most of them.

We do not take a retainer for activity

You pay a platform cost to run the prospecting engine. A performance fee when a deal lands. Not when we send emails. Not when we book a call. When it actually converts.

No lock-in. Ever.

We have never built a lock-in contract. When we can deliver, why would a client leave. Our retention is built on results, not fine print. We do not need a contract to keep you. We need to keep delivering.

Collaborative by default

We run a regular, transparent meeting with every client, walking through campaign performance together and taking strategic decisions based on data. Not human emotion. Not guesswork. Data.

We do not resell SaaS tools

We build solutions. Custom-built for your operations, running on your data, inside your existing systems. When the build is done, you own it. We do not hold it over you.

We do not replace humans with chatbots

AI multiplies what our team can do. It does not replace the human judgement that books the meeting, builds the relationship and earns the trust.

We do not spray and pray

Volume without precision is noise. We build data from scratch for every campaign. Verified decision-makers, mapped by function, reached on the right channel with the right message.

We do not outsource your work

Every person who touches your campaign is on our payroll, answering to our standards. One company. One standard of work. We operate. We do not subcontract.

What we believe

The values we actually work by.

These are not wall posters. They are the things that come up in every client meeting, every campaign review and every decision we make about how to build this business.

VALUE 1

Integrity first

If something is not working, we say so. If the data tells us a different direction, we follow the data. We do not tell clients what they want to hear. We tell them what will move the needle.

VALUE 2

Collaborative by default

We do not disappear after onboarding. We stay in it with you through reviewing performance, adjusting strategy and making decisions together based on what the numbers are telling us.

VALUE 3

Learners before experts

AI is moving faster than any individual can track alone. We invest real time in our own training and our own skills. Because if we stop learning, we stop being useful to our clients.

VALUE 4

Results over retention

We want long client relationships but only because the work earns them. Not because a contract forces them. When we deliver, clients stay. That is the only model we have ever built.
