AI built inside your business. Not around it.

Your control. We build it and hand it over.

Questions we get on every call

The questions we get on every discovery call. Answered honestly.

Where does AI fit in what Automate Accelerator does?
Two places. Inside every Growth Engine campaign as a built-in layer that multiplies what our professional team can do, more reach, more personalisation, more follow-up at scale. And as a standalone AI Solutions engagement where we build AI directly inside your own business operations. Two distinct things. Nothing in between.
Do I need AI Solutions or the Growth Engine?
If you want more qualified meetings and a professional team running your outbound, the Growth Engine. If you have internal workflows costing your team time and money that repeat more than ten times, AI Solutions. If you need both, we can scope both. If neither is right, we will tell you that on the discovery call.
Will AI replace my team?
No. AI handles the repetitive, rules-based layer. Your people focus on the work that requires judgement, relationships and creativity. Every Automate Accelerator engagement is built around your human team, not around replacing it.
How long does it take to build?
Every AI Solutions engagement is scoped individually. You receive a clear timeline as part of the strategy before you commit. What we can tell you is that nothing goes live until you have tested it and approved it.
What does it cost?
AI Solutions is scoped by project. You receive a clear plan and price after the discovery call. No retainer for activity. No surprises after you say yes.
Do you work with businesses outside Australia?
Yes. Automate Accelerator has active engagements across Australia and globally. The system works wherever your buyers are.
What if it does not work?
Every AI Solutions build comes with a testing and proof stage before anything goes live. We do not invoice for outcomes that have not been demonstrated.
