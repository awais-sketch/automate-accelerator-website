The single thing most outbound agencies do badly is data. They rent a list and spray a template, and that is why the meetings are bad. Automate Accelerator builds decision-maker data accurately across every campaign: verified names, verified roles, verified companies, ready for the right message to land.

Why this matters

The right message to the wrong person is wasted time and resources.

Every campaign, every message, every call and every booked meeting traces back to one question: did it reach the right person? For most agencies the answer is no, and that is why their meetings are bad. Our first job in every engagement is making sure the answer is yes.

How we build data

Four steps before a single message goes out.

No shortcuts. No shared lists across clients. No buying an enrichment tool and calling it a strategy.

01

Understand your buyer

We start with who actually makes the buying decision: the industry, the decision-maker title, the company size and the geography. We map this together before anything is built. No assumptions and no guesswork, just a clear picture of your ideal buyer before a single record is pulled.

02

Extract

We pull decision-maker data from named, high-grade enterprise systems. No bulk databases and no recycled lists.

  • LinkedIn Sales Navigator for decision-maker profiles, titles, company size and professional connections.
  • Company websites and records for confirmed contact details and current roles.
  • ASIC and business registry data for verified company structure, size and standing.

Every record is pulled specifically for your campaign and is never shared across clients.

03

Verify

Our team checks every record. Current role confirmed. Email validated. No record enters a campaign until it passes.

04

Update and optimise

People change roles and companies change. Your data stays current. Every active campaign is refreshed continuously so the right person always receives the right message.

The buyer mapping workshop

How every campaign starts. A workshop to map your ideal buyers.

Every campaign starts with a conversation. We call it the data workshop. Before we run a single outreach activity, we sit down with you: a structured conversation with our data team that captures your buyer instinct in a clear brief our team can build from. This is where every engagement begins.

01

Discover

We map your ideal buyer together: the industry, the company size, the geography, the decision-maker title and the signals that tell us this is the right person at the right company at the right time. No assumptions, just a clear brief built from your knowledge of your own market.

02

Sample

We extract a verified sample of real contacts matching your brief. You see exactly who we are targeting before a single message goes out. Real names, real companies and real roles, verified across three sources.

03

Approve

You review the sample and confirm it matches your buyer. Nothing runs until you say yes. Your campaign starts with data you have seen, verified and approved.

Every engagement begins the same way, with a conversation about who your buyer actually is. Everything else follows from that.
